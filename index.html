Method

How it works

The same process in all three situations: before a purchase, after a purchase and before a sale.

Three steps

Interview, check, map.

01

Interview

We talk to the owner, managers and specialists. We ask how the work really runs and who knows what.

02

Check

We compare the answers with each other and with existing documents. If two people describe the same thing differently, we ask again.

03

Map

We write down where the knowledge sits. Each topic gets one of three marks: write down now, automate or in place.

Inside an interview

Each question comes from the last answer.

The person we talk to does not need to install or learn anything. Our team reviews every finding.

InterviewerHow are discounts decided for your five largest customers?
Sales leadMostly I decide. I know what each of them accepted in the past.
InterviewerWhere is that history written down?
Sales leadNowhere, really. Some of it is in old emails.
⚑ Write down now: one person holds the pricing rules. Next we ask the finance lead.
What you get

The knowledge is written down and stays in the company.

  • A knowledge map: who knows what and where it is written down.
  • A list of topics worth writing down now.
  • Repetitive tasks that can be automated.
  • An action plan: each item with an action and an owner.

Written-down knowledge also helps when someone new joins.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does XHive tell us whether to buy?

No. We show how the work really runs and where the knowledge sits. The decision is yours.

Who do you talk to?

The owner, managers and specialists. Who and when is agreed before we start.

What do employees get out of it?

Their knowledge is written down and repetitive work gets simpler. That gives them more time for the work they actually enjoy.

We have documents and a data room. Isn’t that enough?

Documents show what is written down. We add what people know but nobody has written down yet. We use the documents as input.

Why are the interviews not anonymous?

The map has to show who to ask about what. People know why we talk to them before we start. Only the people named in the agreement see names.

Who can see the interviews?

Only the people agreed before we start. Each company’s data is kept separate.

What happens to the result?

The map and the written-down knowledge stay with the company.

Will anyone learn that we are looking at a deal?

We don’t mention your name or your deal without written permission. We sign a confidentiality agreement before we discuss the deal.

Does the AI make decisions?

No. Our team reviews the findings, you make the decisions.

Where is our data stored?

We plan to host in the EU. The exact provider and location go into the agreement.

Is our data used to train AI?

No. Third-party models are not trained on your data.

Are you certified?

Not yet. We are a young company. The table above shows what is done and what is in progress.
